Screen recording isn’t available in this browser

iPadOS doesn’t allow web pages to capture the screen. The built‑in recorder does the same job — no download needed:

  1. Open Control Center (swipe down from the top‑right corner).
  2. Tap the record button, then Start Recording.
  3. Touch and hold the record button first if you want the microphone.
